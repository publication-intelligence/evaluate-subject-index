# Subject-index evaluation V10.1 decision contract

Contract version: `subject-index-evaluation-v10-decision-v2`

Status: adopted successor to V10 decision v1

Decision date: 2026-09-17

This contract preserves the V10 candidate-blind source proof, six dimensions,
ordinary dimension deductions, validity rules, and human-release separation. It
supersedes V10 decision v1 for new calculations because severe or systemic bad
index behavior must materially constrain the numeric result. Existing V10
results are historical inputs, not mechanically relabeled outputs.

## Evidence conservation

Benchmark reconciliation may consolidate source subjects without restoring old
counts, but it must preserve distinct access obligations. The frozen amendment
must carry reconstructable before/after ledgers for benchmark parents, reader
tasks, expected treatments, and weighted access obligations. A reduction greater
than 15% requires an explicit reviewed explanation.

When the candidate fingerprint is unchanged, every earlier major or critical
finding must be dispositioned exactly once as `retained`, `evidence_rebutted`, or
`superseded`. Rebuttal and supersession require evidence and rationale; silence
is not a disposition.

## First-lookup access

Distributed fragments do not establish complete access to an essential
multi-facet synthesis subject. Complete coverage requires all frozen facets to
be satisfied, realistic first-lookup success, and at least one coherent tested
candidate path that satisfies the parent obligation. Broad parents such as a
Great Nation or national-sovereignty synthesis are evaluated by this rule.

An absent warranted cross-reference is measured failed access. It keeps the
cross-reference component applicable at its frozen weight and must not
renormalize that weight onto task or architecture components.

## Distinct harms and path binding

One delivered behavior may reduce multiple dimensions when evidence establishes
different harms: wrong locators reduce reliability, unusable or overbroad routes
reduce findability, and misleading headings reduce conceptual/stance fidelity.
Atomic gate ownership prevents duplicate labels for one predicate; it never
suppresses distinct dimension deductions or systemic-prevalence evaluation.

A major or critical delivered heading-architecture finding that blocks or
misleads must bind the affected delivered `PATH-*`. A node-only attachment is
insufficient for a destructive-route cap and requires re-adjudication.

## Overall-score ceilings

Ordinary dimension arithmetic runs first. Every applicable ceiling is then
evaluated with exact Decimal arithmetic; the lowest ceiling wins. Further
dimension deductions may produce a score below the ceiling.

- An isolated wrong locator without major consequence has no overall ceiling.
- A major destructive route, central omission, or stance reversal caps at 80.
- One systemic root cause affecting at least 10 items, at least 5% of its frozen
  population, at least two sections, and at least 25% of the applicable section
  population caps at 75; at 15% it caps at 60; at 30% it caps at 40.
- Behavior making at least 30% of the candidate locator population unreliable
  caps at 40.
- An empty, unparseable, structurally incomplete, or functionally unusable
  candidate caps at 20.

Every result, report, and projection exposes the pre-cap score, all evaluated
caps, applied cap, thresholds, denominators, evidence IDs, and equations.

## Dominant malformed route

A dominant malformed route is a delivered heading/path that does not identify
one supported complete concept, has at least 25 locators, spans at least 25% of
the source page span, and has independently evidenced major or critical
conceptual and navigation consequences. It caps overall score at 60. It caps at
40 when at least 30% of its applicable locators are unsupported or when the route
contains at least 30% of the candidate locator population. Candidate ambiguity
is judged as delivered and is not evaluator uncertainty.

The Indexia `PATH-EAC65BF97664` (`National`) pattern and Russia
`NODE-21E1763C8A0D` / `PATH-57AD2D32A667` stance evidence are frozen calibration
cases. Their factual judgments must be re-adjudicated in successor artifacts;
this contract does not infer those judgments from labels alone.

## Calibration and audit-integrity cases

The prior Indexia result of 88.52 is provisional. Its observed 701 no-fit
locators across all 17 source units and dominant `National` path must enter the
systemic and dominant-route evaluations. The earlier 71.30→88.52 movement—most
of it from coverage, concept, and findability—shows why preserved findings and
first-lookup obligations cannot silently disappear.

The prior IndexerLabs result of 89.15 is also provisional. Its 410 zero-credit
locators out of 6,222 (including 151 no-fit), spread across all 17 source units,
meet the 5% systemic backstop independent of atomic wrong-locator ownership.
The critical zero-route records—including the missing map route and two
contradictory essential-miss records—plus 76 major conceptual node judgments,
58 major navigation judgments, and two dead-end references require successor
reconciliation and consequence records. These facts are calibration inputs,
not a forced target score.

An optional benchmark parent may remain excluded only while it has no confirmed
major or critical missing-coverage, stance, or first-lookup failure. A confirmed
material failure makes that parent applicable so its axes cannot disappear.

Every major or failed node component requires a corresponding major or critical
defect record in the same dimension. A denominator-level node judgment cannot
silently avoid defect-based consequences.

Structured contradictions block scoring. In particular, `coverage=missing`
cannot coexist with every expected page recorded found; `coverage=complete`
cannot coexist with missed expected pages; and claimed subject/task success
requires at least one matched delivered path. Narrative contradictions that
cannot be resolved deterministically—such as prose claiming an exact usable
route while structured fields say critical failure—require human re-adjudication.
